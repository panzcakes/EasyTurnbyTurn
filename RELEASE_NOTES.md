## Easy Turn by Turn - Google Maps turn-by-turn on the Hero Xpulse 210 cluster

**For the Hero Xpulse 210 only.** Made for and tested only with that bike's cluster; not intended for other bikes or other Hero models.

**Unofficial.** Not made, endorsed or supported by Hero MotoCorp, MapmyIndia (Mappls) or Google.
Experimental: do not look at or use your phone while riding, and do not rely on it as your only navigation.
Use at your own risk.

### Installing: Google Play Protect warning

This app is not in the Play Store, so Google Play Protect may show a warning such as
*"Unknown developer"*, *"App not scanned"* or *"Blocked by Play Protect"*. That means Google does not
know the app, not that it is harmful. The code is open and the app has no internet permission.

To install:
1. Download the APK (`EasyTurnByTurn-v1.2.apk`) below and open it.
2. If your phone asks, allow installing apps from your browser or file manager.
3. On the Play Protect warning, tap **More details > Install anyway**.
4. If there is only an OK button, open Play Store > profile > Play Protect > settings and turn off
   **Scan apps with Play Protect** while you install. You can turn it back on afterwards.

**"App not installed"?** An older copy signed with a different key is on the phone. Uninstall it once
(your settings are lost) and install again. Updates signed with the same key install over each other.

### Notification access: "Restricted setting"

On Android 13 and newer, an app installed outside the Play Store cannot get notification access until you
allow it once. Open **Settings > Apps > Easy Turn by Turn**, tap the three dots at the top right, tap
**Allow restricted settings**, then turn notification access on. This is an Android security feature.

### First use

Pair the bike's cluster in Bluetooth settings, fully close the official Hero app, open the app and follow the
hints above the button, then tap **Turn relay on** and tap the circle to start navigation in Google Maps.
See the README for details.
